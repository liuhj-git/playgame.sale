About Monster Survivors

Monster Survivors is an action-packed survival game where you face waves of increasingly difficult monsters. Your goal is simple: survive as long as possible while defeating hordes of enemies.

With each level, you'll gain new abilities, weapons, and power-ups that will help you fight against the ever-growing monster population. Can you become the ultimate survivor?

This fast-paced game combines elements of roguelike games with arcade action. Each playthrough is unique, with randomized power-ups and enemy spawns making every game a new challenge.

Perfect for quick gaming sessions or extended plays, Monster Survivors offers endless replayability and excitement for gamers of all skill levels.

Game Controls

Movement

  • WASD or Arrow Keys: Move your character
  • Space: Dash/Dodge

Combat
